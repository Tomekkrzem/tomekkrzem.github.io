About Me

Mechanical engineer and robotics researcher interested in building systems that bring together mechanical design, controls, software, and manufacturing.

Education Background

I recently graduated from Northwestern with a combined BS/MS degree in Mechanical Engineering, focusing on robotics and dynamical systems. I built a strong foundation in mechanical design, and engineering as a whole, through hands-on experience across academic, extracurricular and independent projects. As a member to my univeristy's Baja SAE team, I contributed to the design and fabrication of key powertrain components, as well as the general assembly of the off-road vehicle. Outside of this, I also pursued a variety of independent projects, focused on full system integration and software design. All of these skills culminated in my capstone project, in which I designed, machined and assembled a robotic finger from scratch.

Work Experience

I’m a mechanical engineer and robotics researcher who really enjoys building things and figuring out how complex systems work. Most of my experience has involved taking open-ended problems from an initial idea through design, fabrication, programming, and experimental testing. I’ve worked on everything from automated manufacturing equipment and high-speed robotic mechanisms to production tooling, motion-control systems, and ROS-based robotics. I especially enjoy projects where mechanical design and software come together, because they let me work across CAD, machining, electronics, controls, and programming rather than focusing on just one part of a system. I’m looking to keep growing as an engineer and researcher by tackling challenging robotics and automation problems where I can both build real hardware and explore new ideas.
