Overview

This project collection explores how simple local rules can generate complex global behavior. The work progresses from procedural L-system structures and cellular automata to flocking behavior and a larger evolution simulator in which populations change over successive generations.

Each simulation provides a visual, interactive way to study how rule sets, local interactions, and environmental constraints influence the behavior of a larger system.

Challenge

The central challenge was turning compact mathematical or behavioral rules into simulations that remain visually understandable as complexity grows. Each system requires a different representation: recursive rewriting for L-systems, neighborhood rules for cellular automata, local steering rules for boids, and generation-to-generation selection for the evolution simulator.

Beyond implementing the individual rules, the simulations also needed to update many entities efficiently while maintaining a responsive visualization and exposing enough state for the user to understand what the system is doing.
